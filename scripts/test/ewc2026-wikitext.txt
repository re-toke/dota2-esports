'''Esports World Cup 2026''' serves as the final championship of the fourth season of the [[ESL Pro Tour]].  It was originally planned to be hosted in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, but was moved to Paris, France on May 20.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://x.com/EWC_EN/status/2057121960623006162|title=Esports World Cup 2026 is coming to Paris|author=Esports World Cup|author-link=Esports World Cup|date=2026-05-20}}</ref>

:''See [[esports:Esports_World_Cup/2026|here]] to read about the Esports World Cup 2026, the Calendar, the Club Standings and more.''

==Format==
*'''Stage 1: Group Stage''' ''(July 7 - July 12)''
**Four groups of six teams each in round-robin
**All series consist of two games
**{{bgctext|up|Top team}} from each group advance to playoff
**{{bgctext|stayup|2nd,}} {{bgctext|stay|3rd and 4th place teams}} from each group qualify to survival
**{{bgctext|down|Remaining teams}} are eliminated

*'''Stage 2: Survival''' ''(July 14 - July 15)''
**Single-elimination
**4th vs 3rd from groups → winner vs 2nd
**All matches are {{Abbr/Bo3}}
**Four teams advance to playoff

*'''Stage 3: Playoffs''' ''(July 16 - July 19)''
**Single-elimination bracket
**Grand Final is {{Abbr/Bo5}}, all other matches are {{Abbr/Bo3}}
